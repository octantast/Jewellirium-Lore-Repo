## Senders of letters

- Intarsoni Jewelry House: competitors who want to know the source of the gems.
- Taglini school: followers who ask for elemental services.
- Goldfield Priory: asks the player to take part in town life and to show jewelry at an exhibition.
- "too famous to name myself": an actress who may become the face of the house.
- Your secret admirer: three letters with advice and rewards.
- Sotterano and Dottori: patron houses.
- Council of Justice: the warnings and fines.
- Guild of alchemists: the letter that returns the ritual ring.
- Liberum torcular: announcements of days with special effects.

See [events](../events/events.md) for the letters.

The names of the prisoner, the patron and the customers are not given.
