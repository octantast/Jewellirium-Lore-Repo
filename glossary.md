# Glossary

> Source note: the descriptions follow the Android APK build, the most current version. The PC build is slightly older. Exact numbers are not given.

Terms used across the Jewellirium files, in alphabetical order.

Related files: [README](README.md), [day cycle](mechanics/day-cycle.md), [bargaining](mechanics/bargaining.md), [operations](mechanics/operations.md), [alchemy table](mechanics/alchemy-table.md), [basement](mechanics/basement.md), [characters](characters/characters.md), [patron upgrades](upgrades/patron-upgrades.md), [rings](rings/rings.md), [events](events/events.md), [showcase](showcase/showcase.md)

- **Arch:** one of three openings on the offer screens of the patron and the death merchant, each showing one offer. See [patron upgrades](upgrades/patron-upgrades.md).
- **Basement:** the hidden room with the prisoner. Must be open for operations. See [basement](mechanics/basement.md).
- **Bowl full of food:** the feeding option that refills the prisoner's health. See [basement](mechanics/basement.md).
- **Council of Justice:** the authority that sends warning letters after discoveries. See [day cycle](mechanics/day-cycle.md).
- **Customer:** a visitor in the hall with a request and a wallet. See [bargaining](mechanics/bargaining.md).
- **Day:** one workshop cycle with four quarters. See [day cycle](mechanics/day-cycle.md).
- **DEAL:** the confirm button of bargaining, with the operation time. See [bargaining](mechanics/bargaining.md).
- **Undertaker (death merchant):** Nefario. He still sells prisoners; separately, when a prisoner dies, he comes to the hall and leaves a new prisoner of the base level. See [basement](mechanics/basement.md).
- **Discovery:** a visit of the inspector while the basement is open. Three discoveries reset the cycle. See [day cycle](mechanics/day-cycle.md).
- **Element:** Earth, Fire, Water or Air, the groups of the 12 tools. See [bargaining](mechanics/bargaining.md).
- **Marked customer:** a customer on whom the player put a mark for the undertaker; he is scouted and brought at night as a candidate for a new prisoner. A raven sits on him. See [basement](mechanics/basement.md).
- **Returning customer:** a customer with jewelry who comes again with a thumbs-up cloud; a tap gives resources at once and needs a free operation table. See [operations](mechanics/operations.md).
- **Special requirement:** a demand of a customer in the bargaining: an element that is needed or an element that must not be used. See [bargaining](mechanics/bargaining.md).
- **Eye icon:** the button that opens and closes the basement. See [screens](interface/screens.md).
- **Essence:** a feeding option that fills the essence scale . See [basement](mechanics/basement.md).
- **Examination:** a chance during an operation to reveal a scheme. See [operations](mechanics/operations.md).
- **Formula:** an ordered combination of materials in the slots of the alchemy table. See [alchemy table](mechanics/alchemy-table.md).
- **Inspector:** the official who visits the workshop. See [day cycle](mechanics/day-cycle.md).
- **Mail event:** one of 15 letters with a demand or an effect. See [events](events/events.md).
- **Perfect:** the result when the price equals the customer's wallet exactly. Doubles the reputation reward. See [bargaining](mechanics/bargaining.md).
- **Perfect Man:** the goal of the game, a human body fully encrusted with jewels. It is assembled through the crafting progress on the alchemy table: the schemes fill in with every found formula (counter x/61). See [README](README.md).
- **Patron:** the seller of upgrades for the workshop and the hall. The envelope on the workshop table is the button of communication with him. See [patron upgrades](upgrades/patron-upgrades.md).
- **Prisoner:** the person in the basement who synthesizes materials. See [basement](mechanics/basement.md).
- **Ritual ring:** a PC-build addition; the mobile version has no rings. The ring holds magic stones. See [rings](rings/rings.md).
- **Showcase:** the display of pieces in the hall: 3 open slots at the start and 3 closed ones that open through upgrades. The player puts unlocked pieces on it and takes them off by taps. Every piece gives its own coins, reputation and customer waiting time, and the coins and reputation arrive at the end of the day. See [showcase](showcase/showcase.md).
- **Yellow outline:** marks a workshop object that is a button opening its own screen. See [screens](interface/screens.md).
- **Time-lapse:** the button that runs the day three times faster. See [day cycle](mechanics/day-cycle.md).
- **Triangle (trine):** three tools of one element chosen together; gives a bonus. See [bargaining](mechanics/bargaining.md).
- **Customer cloud:** the cloud at the feet of a customer. Tapping it opens the customer screen; its look is the indicator of the waiting timer: white is normal waiting, rain and a storm mean that the patience is running out. See [screens](interface/screens.md).
- **Mirror of the sky:** the round frame with a window at the lower right of the hall. It shows the time of day by colour (yellow day, darker evening and night, pinkish morning) and is not a button. See [screens](interface/screens.md).
- **Lowering lift:** the crate that rises and lowers in a recess of the wall behind the cabinet at the upper right of the workshop, a hidden descent down to the basement. A tap opens the prisoner screen for passing food down. The button is hidden while the basement is closed. See [screens](interface/screens.md).
