# Patron upgrades

> Source note: the descriptions follow the Android APK build, the most current version. The PC build is slightly older. Exact numbers are not given; this is a rough list of the upgrades.

The patron brings upgrades of the workshop and the hall for coins and reputation. The envelope on the workshop table opens his screen. Upgrades are an addition to the core gameplay.

Related files: [README](../README.md), [characters](../characters/characters.md), [alchemy table](../mechanics/alchemy-table.md), [bargaining](../mechanics/bargaining.md), [operations](../mechanics/operations.md), [basement](../mechanics/basement.md), [showcase](../showcase/showcase.md), [events](../events/events.md), [screens](../interface/screens.md), [glossary](../glossary.md)

## How the offer works

- The screen is titled "PATRON'S OFFER" and has three arches, each showing one upgrade on a scroll.
- Each offer has a price in coins and a price in reputation. The coin price is spent; the reputation price is only a requirement. The DEAL button shows the days the patron needs to bring the item.
- One order at a time: after an upgrade is ordered, its arch gets a RESERVED seal, buying the others is blocked until the patron delivers the current one, and the screen says on which day it will be delivered.
- Some upgrades unlock the next upgrade of the same line.
- The events "Tension between patrons' houses", "Sotterano" and the secret admirer letter about a free upgrade change the offer ([events](../events/events.md)).

## Rough list of the upgrades

| Area | Upgrades |
|---|---|
| Alchemy table | more slots (light, extension, new tools); the sealed slots open this way |
| Examination | lenses (the examination cloud stays longer), painkillers (examination comes more often) |
| Basement | antiseptics (more health of the prisoner), doubled extraction of resources |
| Operations | surgical tables (more operation tables), healing agents (faster operations) |
| Hall and showcase | showcases and entourage of the hall (more showcase slots), a corner for one more customer, showcases to attract wealthier people |
| Income | luxuries in the hall (more profit), the approval plate and the portrait of the patron (more reputation) |
| Patron | contract to supply offers a day faster |
| Inspector | blindness of justice, no fines |

The game page and the screens use these texts: "alchemical table light", "showcases and entourage of the hall change" (icon "+1 slot"), "blindness of justice. no fines".

## Reset

After three discoveries the progress of the cycle is reset, while the unlocked jewelry is kept ([day cycle](../mechanics/day-cycle.md)).
