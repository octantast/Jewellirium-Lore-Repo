# Mail events

> Source note: the descriptions follow the Android APK build, the most current version. The PC build is slightly older. This list comes from the PC scripts and is an addition to the core gameplay; the letters can differ in the mobile build. Exact numbers are not given.

Mail events are letters that give the player a demand with a deadline, a day with a special effect, or a change in the patron's offers. The PC scripts have 15 of them. The letters are signed with the family name GIOIELLIERI where a family name is used.

## When events arrive

Events come with pauses of a few days between them. An event is chosen at random, and the state of the workshop (coins, reputation, the prisoner, the patron's offers) makes some events more likely. The last three events are not repeated at once.

## The 15 events

| No. | Sender and title | Demand or effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intarsoni Jewelry House (competitors) | Hand over gems by a deadline, or lose reputation. The letter comes in three steps |
| 2 | Taglini school | Provide services of one named element by a deadline. Reward: reputation |
| 3 | Goldfield Priory | Spend coins by a deadline. Refusal: a fine |
| 4 | "too famous to name myself" (an actress) | Reach a reputation level by a deadline. Reward: showcase bonuses are raised for some days |
| 5 | Goldfield Priory | Create new pieces of jewelry by a deadline. Reward: reputation |
| 6 | Your secret admirer | Make perfect deals in a row. Reward: a free upgrade from the patron |
| 7 | Your secret admirer | Hold a sum of money by a deadline. Reward: food costs nothing. Refusal: a fine |
| 8 | Your secret admirer | Earn coins from deals by a deadline. Reward: reputation |
| 9 | Eclipse ("Liberum torcular") | A day when the services of the elements take little time (and magic stones are not damaged in the PC build) |
| 10 | Town Day ("Liberum torcular") | More customers regardless of reputation |
| 11 | Tension between patrons' houses ("Liberum torcular") | The patron is unavailable for some days. The letter names the houses of Dottori and Sotterano |
| 12 | Sotterano | Deals with the patron do not require reputation |
| 13 | Black pox ("Liberum torcular") | Deals with the undertaker do not require reputation |
| 14 | Fog ("Liberum torcular") | Nothing can be seen outside the windows |
| 15 | Mourning ("Liberum torcular") | No customers |

- Letters 1 to 8 are written in the voice of a person or a house. Letters 9 to 11 and 13 to 15 are announcements signed "Liberum torcular".
- The rewards are given when the demand is met.
