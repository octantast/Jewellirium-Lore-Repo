# Operations

> Source note: the descriptions follow the Android APK build, the most current version. The PC build is slightly older. Exact numbers are not given.

An operation is the encrusting procedure the player performs on a customer after a deal. It runs on an operation table (a bed) and ends with the payment.

Related files: [README](../README.md), [day cycle](day-cycle.md), [bargaining](bargaining.md), [alchemy table](alchemy-table.md), [basement](basement.md), [patron upgrades](../upgrades/patron-upgrades.md), [glossary](../glossary.md)

## Course of an operation

1. After a deal the customer moves to a free operation table. The number of tables grows with the upgrades "surgical table".
2. The duration depends on the piece and on the services chosen in the bargaining ([bargaining](bargaining.md)). Upgrades make operations faster.
3. The timer runs only during the day and only while the basement is open. The tutorial: "without an open basement it is impossible to start encrusting. hidden samples must be seen during the process".
4. The timer follows the time-lapse button ([day cycle](day-cycle.md)).
5. At the end the player receives the coins and the reputation of the deal, and the customer leaves.

No free table: when all operation tables are occupied, the bargaining screen shows "NO FREE TABLES" instead of the DEAL button and the table requirement is red ([screens](../interface/screens.md)).

Materials: the piece needs metals, precious stones and semi-precious stones ([alchemy table](alchemy-table.md)). They are spent at the deal. Without them the game says: "more gems and metals need to be synthesized to recreate a piece of jewelry and incrust it".

## Examination

- During the operation a cloud can appear at random above the table. It has to be tapped quickly. A tap means that an idea of a new piece came, and a hint of the new formula starts to assemble on the alchemy table, for example that the formula begins with a metal (author).
- The tutorial: "sometimes there is an opportunity to perform an examination during encrusting. it reveals schemes of correct combinations".
- The chance of an examination and the time the cloud stays visible grow with upgrades (painkillers, lenses) ([patron upgrades](../upgrades/patron-upgrades.md)).
- The first examination of the game appears at once as part of the tutorial.

## Returning customers

A customer who already has jewelry can come again with a thumbs-up cloud. Such a customer needs no bargaining. A tap on the cloud gives resources at once, like a quick examination, and needs a free operation table ([bargaining](bargaining.md), [screens](../interface/screens.md)).

## Dissatisfied customers

The tutorial: "serve the customers as quickly as possible while they wait patiently. A DISSATISFIED customer CAN cause trouble".
