# Rings

> Source note: the descriptions follow the Android APK build, the most current version. The newer mobile version has no rings at all. This file describes an addition to the gameplay of the older PC build. Exact stats are not given.

In the PC build the player has a ritual ring with magic stones. Each stone gives one effect while it sits in the ring. The rings are not part of the core gameplay.

Related files: [README](../README.md), [day cycle](../mechanics/day-cycle.md), [basement](../mechanics/basement.md), [events](../events/events.md), [glossary](../glossary.md)

## How the ring is obtained (PC build)

A letter from the guild of alchemists returns the ritual ring that the player sent for revision. The letter says that any stone inserted into the ring can be used indefinitely, but stones wear out when they are changed, so they should be changed as little as possible. After it the mail events begin ([events](../events/events.md)).

## How stones appear (PC build)

The tutorial: "use the magic of the ring to make things easier. don't miss the moment of the day when the corresponding stone is synthesized in the basement". A crystal cloud appears in the basement once per day, and a tap on it gives a random stone.

## Effects (English strings of the game)

| Ring | Effect in the game text |
|---|---|
| Sun | Prisoner's health drops slower |
| Moon | The inspector's visit time can be seen |
| Venus | Unhappy customers don't lower reputation |
| Mars | Resources are not deducted on a failed craft in part of the cases |
| Mercury | Resources are not deducted on a failed incrustation in part of the cases |

## Other rings in the strings

The English strings also name the rings of Jupiter, Uranus, Saturn, Pluto and Neptune with effects on limits, advertising and the speed of time. They are not in the mobile version, and how they are obtained is not documented here.

The set name "Magic of Rings" belongs to the showcase ([showcase](../showcase/showcase.md)).
