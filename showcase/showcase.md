# Showcase

> Source note: the descriptions follow the Android APK build, the most current version. The PC build is slightly older. Exact numbers are not given.

The showcase is a display in the hall where the player puts jewelry pieces. The tutorial: "put in a showcase jewelry you want to offer to customers in the first place. the better the design, the longer it keeps visitors and the more bonuses it brings".

Related files: [README](../README.md), [day cycle](../mechanics/day-cycle.md), [patron upgrades](../upgrades/patron-upgrades.md), [events](../events/events.md), [alchemy table](../mechanics/alchemy-table.md), [screens](../interface/screens.md), [glossary](../glossary.md)

## Slots

- The screen is titled "Showcase design". The showcase starts with 3 open slots and 3 closed slots that open through patron upgrades (author). The scroll at the left shows the open slots ([screens](../interface/screens.md)).
- The order of filling the slots does not matter (author).
- **Controls (author):** the right half of the screen is a strip of the unlocked pieces. The player swipes it and taps the chosen piece to put it on the showcase. A tap on a piece that is already on the showcase clears the slot and returns the piece to the collection. Only unlocked pieces can be put on it.

## Income

- The showcase pays coins and reputation once per day, at the start of the night ([day cycle](../mechanics/day-cycle.md)). The screen says "money and reputation will be received by the end of this day".
- Author's description of the loop: new pieces replace the old weak ones on the showcase for bigger passive bonuses at the end of the day. The player chooses which pieces to take off and which to put on.
- Three indicators run along the bottom of the screen: scales with a heart (a bonus to the waiting time of customers: with a full showcase they admire it and stay longer), a coin (coins by the end of the day) and a cup (reputation by the end of the day).
- Every piece gives its own bonuses (author): different pieces give different bonuses. The game shows the sum to the player.
- The event "too famous to name myself" raises the showcase bonuses for a while ([events](../events/events.md)).
